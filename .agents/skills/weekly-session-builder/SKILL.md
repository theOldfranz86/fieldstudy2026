# Weekly Session Builder

Build a French-language session that advances one concrete project decision and produces observable evidence.

## Read first

Read the root YAML specifications, `curriculum/PEDAGOGICAL_FRAMEWORK.md`, `teaching-materials/slides/SLIDE_DESIGN.md`, the source registry and the relevant weekly reading-map entry.

## Required outputs

Create or revise, in the relevant `teaching-materials/slides/week-NN/` folder:

1. `SESSION_PLAN.md`
2. `SLIDE_BRIEF.md`
3. `COACHING_GUIDE.md`
4. `DELIVERABLE.md`
5. `QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`

Do not create a PPTX unless the user separately requests it after these files are approved.

## Workflow

1. Resolve week ID, date, outcomes, milestone, expected output and preparation for the following week.
2. Place the week in the sequential design: framing, qualitative phase, qualitative-to-questionnaire integration, Sphinx/quantitative phase, analysis, recommendations or oral.
3. Plan exactly 180 minutes, including transitions, group setup, restitution and closure.
4. Mark Concrete Experience, Reflective Observation, Abstract Conceptualization and Active Experimentation explicitly.
5. Limit methodological input to concepts used in the same session; normally keep the briefing within 30–60 minutes.
6. Design workshop, coaching rotation for about 9 groups, feedback, debriefing and an observable deliverable.
7. Record individual accountability and the link to the next week.
8. Attribute external material through `SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml` and keep original course materials separate.

## Sequential-method checks

- Before W06, require at least one interview per student and a de-identified thematic evidence matrix.
- At W06, require traceability from qualitative themes, language, attributes, hypotheses and divergent cases to questionnaire items.
- At W07–W09, use Sphinx and plan for at least 300 complete usable responses plus quality control.
- At W10–W11, distinguish results from interpretation and integrate qualitative and quantitative evidence.
- Never introduce a written-report requirement.

## Readiness

The package is ready only if objectives, activities, output, feedback and next-week preparation align; all four Kolb phases are explicit; workload fits 180 minutes; and no client or institutional information is invented.
