# Milestone Manager

Use observable evidence to support timely, proportionate interventions.

## Workflow

1. Read the milestone in `MILESTONES.yaml` and verify its date against `COURSE_SCHEDULE.yaml`.
2. Confirm that every group submitted before the session; record the submission time when it is defined.
3. Use the 60-minute checkpoint in the same session; 2–3 groups selected by the instructors present.
4. Give oral feedback to presenters and written feedback to every group.
5. Test artifacts against the documented quality gate.
6. Assign exactly one outcome: `approved`, `approved_with_revision` or `suspended`.
7. For `approved_with_revision`, set a revision deadline within 7 calendar days.
8. For `suspended`, block the next phase until approval and specify the smallest feasible recovery action.

## Method-specific checks

- M01: problem framing, desk research, research question and team charter.
- M02: one interview per student, de-identified qualitative synthesis, consent/data note and traceability to questionnaire v1.
- M03: Sphinx dataset, progress toward or attainment of at least 300 complete usable responses, quality control, data dictionary and analysis plan.
- M04: integrated findings, recommendation package, oral readiness and individual contribution record; no written report.

## Review rules

- Distinguish missing from weak artifacts.
- Do not infer consent, contribution, data quality or sample attainment without documentation.
- Preserve quality gates; do not lower them solely to meet a deadline.
- Escalate ethical, confidentiality or participant-safety concerns through the confirmed institutional route.
