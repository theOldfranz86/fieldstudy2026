# Slide Deck Builder

Create concise, activity-centred decks that support the weekly decision without replacing workshop, project work or coaching.

## Production gate

Read and require:

- the four root YAML specifications and selected week;
- `curriculum/PEDAGOGICAL_FRAMEWORK.md`;
- `teaching-materials/slides/SLIDE_DESIGN.md`;
- `teaching-materials/readings/SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml` and the weekly reading map;
- approved `SESSION_PLAN.md`, `SLIDE_BRIEF.md`, `COACHING_GUIDE.md`, `DELIVERABLE.md` and `QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`.

If a required file is absent or the pedagogical review is `not_ready`, stop and report the missing revision. Do not infer a full session from the schedule alone.

## Workflow

1. Build the narrative from the approved brief: opening, objectives, course position, experience, reflection, targeted concept, application, deliverable, next week and sources.
2. Use one main learning action or decision per slide and make success criteria visible before activities.
3. Add speaker notes with objective, Kolb phase, explanation, duration, student question, transition and full source.
4. Follow the 16:9 design system and the supplied HES-SO reference deck without copying it wholesale or inventing official brand rules.
5. Distinguish original, adapted, cited and simulated content.
6. Render and inspect the PPTX before release.

## Content and safety rules

- Use French for learner-facing content.
- Include questions, instructions and debriefing; avoid an exclusively frontal sequence.
- Do not add theory unused in the same session.
- Do not invent Maison Maye information or demonstrative data without labelling them simulated.
- Do not expose personal data, identifiable transcripts, raw data or client-confidential information.
- Attribute author, title, licence and source for adapted content.
- Do not add a written-report deliverable.

## Final checks

Verify alignment with the five planning files, the sequential research design, the relevant milestone, source rules, accessibility, speaker notes and visual rendering.
